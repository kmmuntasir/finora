## How the SVGs were made

- Shapes traced with potrace at 4× supersampling from colour-separated masks.
- The icon fill is a fitted linear gradient (five stops) plus a second
  gradient for the darker wave, clipped to the icon shape.
- The jellyfish is negative space (transparent), as in the concept, so it
  shows the background colour.
- Rendered output differs from the PNG by about 2/255 per pixel on average,
  only along anti-aliased edges.
- The wordmark is outlined paths; its typeface is not identified.
